# Collection

Build a browse-and-collect surface for a researcher assembling a reference set of physical objects from multiple sellers and archives.

## Primary job

The researcher browses mixed records, saves candidates, compares selected objects, opens provenance/seller detail, handles a changed availability state, and returns to the collection without losing comparison context.

## Protected product contract

- R1: Object identity, provenance, seller/archive source, dimensions, and availability remain distinct semantic roles.
- R2: Saved items can be compared without forcing every record into identical card grammar.
- R3: Opening provenance or seller detail and returning preserves selected comparison items and browse position.
- R4: Availability changes do not silently remove an item from the saved collection.
- R5: Missing price, unknown maker, long provenance, and mixed record types remain legible.
- R6: Keyboard users can browse, save, compare, inspect source detail, and return.
- R7: Narrow layouts preserve collection and comparison continuity.

The surface should feel authored for collecting and provenance work, not like a generic ecommerce template.
