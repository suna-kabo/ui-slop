# Casewire

Build a production-ready investigative research workspace for analysts tracing claims across sources.

## Primary job

An analyst opens a claim, inspects supporting and contradicting evidence, follows a source into detail, records a judgment, and returns to the exact research context without reconstructing filters or selection.

## Protected product contract

- R1: A claim can be selected from a mixed evidence set and its relationships are understandable without relying on color alone.
- R2: Supporting, contradicting, and unresolved evidence remain distinguishable even when titles are similar.
- R3: Opening a source detail and returning preserves claim selection, active filters, and the analyst's place.
- R4: A judgment note can be saved and remains attached to the correct claim after reload.
- R5: Missing author/date values and unusually long source titles do not break hierarchy or hide provenance.
- R6: Keyboard users can reach claim, evidence, source detail, note, and return actions.
- R7: Narrow layouts preserve the current claim and next action rather than flattening everything into a generic feed.

## Content character

This is dense investigative work. Users compare evidence, provenance, uncertainty, and chronology repeatedly. Do not turn it into a marketing dashboard. Use the supplied fixture as the content source.
