# UI Slop v3 rendered quality investigation

## Scope and baseline

The preserved baseline is v3.0.1 at `3a2e5468a79e5ef5a5bdb3e52445d0fca99cf903`. Its Git archive SHA-256 is `d4c56a12321356ba0af72b08635073017c040d99022cac721de5b7057daba6c1`. All 25 baseline tests passed with Node 24.22.1, Playwright 1.63.0 and Debian Chromium 156.0.8078.79. The stock Playwright executable was absent. Setting `UI_SLOP_CHROMIUM_PATH=/usr/bin/chromium` exercised the suite with an installed browser.

The supplied attachment contained the investigation brief. The workspace attachment areas, repository tree, all available branch history and remote heads contained no original Kilnroom ZIP, screenshots, source, or review record. The experiment therefore includes independently rebuilt kiln apps and a controlled fixture that can isolate symptoms. Historical causation and whether the original reviewer saw specific states remain unverified.

## Experimental design

A used the frozen skill. B changed only calibration. C changed only the review protocol. D included both. The [preparation script](prepare-slices.mjs) reproduces the exact files. All four received the same two [briefs](slice-briefs.md), static output format, shared Chromium access and roughly twelve minutes of active work per context. Codex CLI failed to initialize on its read-only runtime directory. Fresh delegated contexts used the same configuration available in this chat. No seed, temperature, or exact spend controls were exposed. There is one generation per cell and shared infrastructure. Generator contexts were instructed to avoid all sibling artifacts. The review copies exclude the generator's notes and hide generating condition.

The controlled review challenge uses eight variants of one kiosk-free studio booking flow. Variant differences are limited to copy structure, hierarchy/status affordance, absence of cancellation, focus continuity, delayed response, weak detail, and an unusual visual direction with a mobile usability cost. The conventional control protects against rejecting a familiar solution by default. The reviewer pair operated the same opaque cases independently. The old protocol was tested under favorable conditions because both reviewers began without the generator rationale. One reviewer per protocol leaves reviewer identity confounded with protocol. The fixture test establishes defect observability rather than statistical detection rates.

### Controlled Quality Gate challenge

Both independent reviewers marked the conventional case READY and all seven variants NOT_READY. Both found missing cancellation after reload, lost keyboard continuation, silent response, hard-to-read operational type, overly prominent supporting copy, poor detail typography, and the narrow two-column usability cliff. The baseline reviewer additionally caught that Back could close an active load and then allow a delayed selection to reopen detail and steal focus. The experimental reviewer produced operated alternatives for the hierarchy, mobile composition, and detail quality vetoes, and preserved serif for the hierarchy example at a smaller scale. Neither rejected the acceptable conventional flow. Neither assigned numerical aesthetic scores. This comparison provides no evidence the revised protocol detects more defects than a diligent baseline reviewer. It supports collecting a bounded comparison when disagreement concerns the correction, and delaying rationale to protect initial observation.

The missing cancellation and violated keyboard return contract are Evidence Gate hard failures. Silent delayed feedback, poor comparative readability, and weak mobile composition are qualitative vetoes under this brief. The conventional control passes the fixture contract and reviewers accepted it. It is an evaluation control and cannot certify a complete product.

The reconstruction can reliably exercise cross-day occupancy, named capacity contributors and cancellation. The historical concerns about contradictory statuses and opaque aggregates remain historical hypotheses. A reconstructed app exposing or avoiding the same symptoms can establish how the v3 workflow behaves in a new run, but cannot establish what happened to the missing original.

### Full kiln workflows

Fresh contexts produced two browser-local products from the original full kiln brief, using frozen v3.0.1 and the clean installed revision. They separately proved a core slice, then built member intake and tracking, material validation and recovery, staff scheduling, manifests, load assignment and reversal, progress, and demand. Both received the same follow-up request to exercise below-manifest capacity and cross-day scheduling. This rounds out the full workflow, while the prototypes' shared data remain within one browser profile. Running a multi-user service or integrating a physical kiln was outside this experiment.

The baseline uses a stronger active-firing view and quantifies shelf area and height limits. The candidate presents a comparable staff calendar with explicit carryover and an event-by-event member history. Both still rely on a large serif heading and a sidebar on desktop. The baseline's mobile active-firing graphic gives one current firing priority over the schedule. The candidate's dated list helps scan multiple loads, but quarter-width desktop cards use small status and capacity type. These are different product priorities within a common visual vocabulary.

Blinded operation proved submission retry and persistence, assignment and reversal, member-visible progress, and overlap rejection in both. It also identified two material consistency defects that separate happy-path runs had missed. In the baseline, a custom 300 cm² cap correctly yielded 44 cm² free with 256 cm² in the manifest, while two surfaces still showed a 4,200 cm² denominator. In the candidate, the calendar correctly carried a still-cooling load into Thursday but its detail and overlap error described the older Wednesday end. The pre-review candidate had also exposed a seeded Thursday collision in the rendered calendar. This was corrected before the blind copy was made. The frozen comparison remains in the evidence bundle. Later corrections are isolated and evaluated separately.

A separate keyboard follow-up reproduced the frozen candidate’s skip link replacing the member route with the staff schedule at both 1440 and 320px. The correction focused the main region and kept the member URL and heading at both widths.

The independent correction retest passed both material repros at 1440 and 390px. The 300 cm² override agreed across summary, detail and assignment, survived reload, preserved the neighboring load, and recovered when raised to 500 cm². The candidate calendar, detail and blocked creation agreed that cooling occupied the kiln through at least Nov 19, while the plan still showed Nov 17–18. Moving to Nov 20 saved, marking the load ready released the extension, and the neighboring planned hold stayed enforced.

The candidate also replaced duplicate submission mismatch copy with one field-associated explanation and moved focus to the relevant control. Generator Chromium retests at 1440, 768 and 320px verified stage and glaze errors, saved draft, corrected fields and submission. The frozen independent comparison captures the initial failure. The final candidate keeps the targeted correction separately from the frozen trial.

### Dense workflow and fingerprint check

A fresh original context and a fresh installed-skill context built browser-local reading rooms from the same natural newsroom brief. Both retained six representative source records and three claims with simultaneous source register, reading area and claim context on desktop. Their mobile information architecture differed. The baseline offers Sources, Reading and Claims tabs. The candidate keeps the active transcript first and opens the register on demand. Both preserve source-specific material and tested evidence links across reload. The candidate's bounded comparison repair reordered attribution and one excerpt from each source before the remaining transcripts. The narrow version exposed the second source heading in the first viewport.

The blind reviewer followed search, passage, claim, status, comparison, return, reload, and undo in both. The baseline distinguished supporting, qualifying and contradicting evidence and opened exact compared passages. Its selected comparison reset on reopening, and two save actions lost keyboard focus. The candidate kept quoted provenance and exact citations in the claim ledger, and its tested status and citation flows held focus. Its comparison operated at document level, so a relevant secondary excerpt could sit behind “More passages.” Both passed 320px checks for long titles, source selection and empty evidence. This shows transfer of the correction procedure to a dense product and leaves a real editorial tradeoff in the result.

The consumer discovery experiments used date-led show bills, image-led features, and documentary artwork. Operational schedules used calendar bars, grouped lists, or role-specific views. The dense tools used document-led layouts and explicit citations. There is no single imposed aesthetic, although the two full kiln builds and both dense builds independently reused prominent serif titles, a pale workspace, and multi-column desktop shells. Domain relevance does not establish that these conventions are always well proportioned. Mobile visual and interaction differences persisted with the same tool budget. The sample cannot establish a long-run fingerprint rate.
