# Equipment desk reviewer challenge

The prototype tracks a loan for camera kit EQ-18. Pickup had been reserved for 12:00. A person arrives early at 10:20 and the desk operator issues the kit. The booked time, actual issue and return deadline serve different purposes. Equipment inventory helps a second operator tell what is physically at the desk. The item remains checked out across reload until its issue is reversed.

Open `checkout.html?case=62` and `checkout.html?case=75` in separate browser contexts. Begin at the pickup desk, operate the early issue, confirm what the operator sees, then check whether colleague-facing availability, reload and reversal protect the contract. Save observations with viewport, step, state and screenshots or a trace. Judge whether times describe different events or conflict, and treat a false claim of availability as an Evidence Gate failure. This is a separate post-experiment challenge of the review procedure.
