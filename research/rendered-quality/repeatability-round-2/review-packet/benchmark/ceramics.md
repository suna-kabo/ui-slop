# Mill Creek Firing Desk

Mill Creek is a volunteer-run ceramics studio. Build a browser-local application for members and shift leads to schedule, share and track electric kiln firings. Assume the operating date is May 12, 2027. Use the same state for both roles and preserve it across reloads. Seed the following records and add only content that helps operate the studio.

| Kiln and load | Service window | Program | Capacity and contents |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Alder / MC-14 | May 12 16:00 to May 14 09:00 | Cone 06 bisque | 3,000 cm², 28 cm maximum height. Bea's slip cups use 1,650 cm² and are queued. |
| Ash / MC-15 | May 13 08:00 to May 14 16:00 | Cone 6 glaze | 2,400 cm², 23 cm maximum height. Suri's blue bowls use 2,100 cm² and are queued. |
| Alder / MC-16 | May 14 12:00 to May 15 18:00 | Cone 6 glaze | 3,000 cm², 28 cm maximum height. No work assigned. |

Member Jules has a bisque tray at 800 cm² × 8 cm and a glaze pitcher at 500 cm² × 24 cm. Members can inspect occupancy across May 12–15, see load contents and remaining usable shelf area, submit either piece to a compatible load, see why an attempted submission cannot proceed, withdraw queued work, and see progress or pickup status. They can also register another piece with name, program, shelf area and height. A submission occupies capacity immediately and remains visible to the lead. A withdrawal restores capacity. Repeated submissions must avoid double counting.

Shift leads can inspect all loads, add a firing window and choose the kiln and program, prevent time overlaps on the same kiln, inspect the manifest, remove a queued piece, and advance a load through queued, firing, cooling and pickup. Capacity, progress and member status must agree across both roles. Moving focus, returning from detail, changing roles or reloading should preserve the context needed to continue. The studio uses local time and no backend or real kiln integration.
