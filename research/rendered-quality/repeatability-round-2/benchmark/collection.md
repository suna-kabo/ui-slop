# Coast & Current

Build a browser-local guide to small coastal outings for people choosing a half-day trip. Someone may browse several places in a session, save a shortlist and return later. Each place needs a relevant image and useful distinctions between the setting, effort, access and visit logistics. The experience should work on a phone while planning outdoors.

| Place | Setting | Visit facts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Breakwater Steps | Harbor | 35 min, stairs, open daily, bus 4 to Pier Road, best at slack tide |
| Salt Reed Loop | Wetland | 55 min, step-free boardwalk, open daily, bus 6 to Marsh Gate, bring binoculars |
| Lark Cove | Beach | 80 min, loose shingle, weekends only, limited parking, reach only at low tide |
| Old Pilot House | Harbor | 45 min, steep approach, Thu–Sun, bus 4 to Pilot Lane, staffed exhibit |
| Dune Glass Trail | Beach | 70 min, sandy grades, open daily, bus 11 to Dune Road, dogs on lead |
| Dovetail Hide | Wetland | 25 min, step-free path, Tue–Sun, no bus, closes at dusk |
| Gull Point | Harbor | 60 min, level pavement, open daily, bus 6 to Ferry Yard, exposed to wind |
| Ebb Pools | Beach | 50 min, slippery rocks, open daily, no bus, check tide before setting out |

People can browse, filter by setting and step-free access, sort by visit duration or name, and see meaningful result counts. Detail has a photograph or authored illustration and the facts needed to decide whether to go. People can save and remove places in browse, detail or shortlist, with immediate consistent feedback and persistence across reload. They can open two different places in succession and return to the originating result with filters, order, scroll position and keyboard continuation preserved. An empty result should make recovery possible. Keep the app useful without external accounts or services.
