If an open design decision materially changes what people can read, compare or operate, render a materially different alternative beside the current hypothesis before selecting the direction. Reuse the same difficult content and task. Inspect the opening and active states at working desktop and mobile sizes, and operate the action that distinguishes the options. Record which observation changed or confirmed the selection and one tradeoff that remains. Limit the comparison to the affected region or sequence. Skip a comparison when the product or an established pattern already settles the decision.
