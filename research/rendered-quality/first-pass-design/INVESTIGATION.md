# First-pass design judgment investigation

**Recommendation: `KEEP_EXPERIMENTAL`.** The follow-up clause reliably elicited rendered alternatives. It did not reliably produce a better selected proof than frozen v3.0.1. Original v3.0.1 led 5–3 overall in the selected-proof blind comparisons. The stable runtime stays at the original revision.

This study compares the first runnable concept under three configurations before any implementation review. It follows the [Round 2 repeatability investigation](https://github.com/suna-kabo/ui-slop/blob/d58f873e729a540b6254b459fdd10281685af668/research/rendered-quality/repeatability-round-2/INVESTIGATION.md) and the [earlier rendered-quality work](https://github.com/suna-kabo/ui-slop/blob/408395de3f3c1c9e6e121bf1b0ced744e253e280/research/rendered-quality/INVESTIGATION.md). Those experimental revisions remain separately available. The investigation branch started from frozen stable v3.0.1, `3a2e5468a79e5ef5a5bdb3e52445d0fca99cf903`.

## Why Round 2 converged

I inspected the archived Initial contact sheet before opening the generator rationales, then replayed the frozen apps and queried actual glyph fonts using Chromium. All twelve openings drew their main heading in Georgia at desktop and mobile sizes. Desktop heading sizes ranged from 54 to 89.28px at 1440px, and mobile from 34 to 52px at 390px. The evidence includes all sixty audited opening states and their metrics. The pattern crossed both skill assignments and all three briefs. A dark Riverwatch run is a genuine palette exception. The status sizes and composition differed even where the heading face matched.

The common type choice was sometimes suitable. A serif title and authored cover-like illustrations help Coast & Current communicate outing character. On `60122cbb0c` and `fc2950d5d4`, successive display, scope and browse zones left little concrete trip information visible on entry. On `0eb8ac9098` the dominant title consumed vertical space while the manifest and remaining area did the useful work. On Riverwatch, prominent titles for the station can help establish the selected context, but a repeated station name makes sample ID and time indispensable. The more consequential problem is what the heading displaces, which important values demand a second scan, and what remains visible during the second action.

| Hypothesis | Evidence and limit |
| --- | --- |
| The skill directly specifies the look | Frozen `visual-direction.md` and `ai-fingerprint-gate.md` explicitly flag generic paper/editorial shells. The stable text offers no recurring serif, Georgia, off-white, or microtype requirement. The baseline concept reference allows alternative models, while the typography checks call for a materially different direction and actual rendering. That guidance was permissive about when exploration was useful. |
| The category and model predispose the look | Generators wrote that print-like type suited places and that warm serif would make the kiln ledger feel editorial. Other rationales chose system sans for data and serif for display. Similar language appears across conditions. The fresh no-skill controls demonstrate that the model can independently pick both editorial paper and dark operator palettes. The controls use the same CLI system prompt and model defaults. They cannot isolate pretraining, developer instructions, or fine-tuning. |
| Reference calibration imported the style | The Round 2 records cited native selects, browser history, table conventions and domain objects. They repeatedly say live external reference inspection was unavailable under the local-material constraint. The logs do not support external sites as the source. Unevaluated analogies such as field guides, paper ledgers or print fairs could still supply category associations. That influence was not independently randomized. |
| A direction was selected before working alternatives | `4a749aeccf` discussed gallery versus illustrated index, `95ecf55b80` discussed modal versus split detail, and `0eb8ac9098` discussed agenda versus board. Their notes explain the choice in prose and record operating the selected model. Those notes do not establish an operated type/proportion counterexample. The first new trial reproduced this failure mode even with a conditional preselection comparison sentence. |
| Browser capture was too late | Round 2’s browser proof checked legitimate task risks. Full-product review then reported oversized openings and trims that brought facts into view. For example, `894aed0fda` removed a workspace eyebrow, subtitle and mast descriptor so the last sample row appeared in the first desktop frame. This is evidence that browser review found a problem after the composition was committed. It says little about why the first hypothesis was chosen. |

The strongest supported combination is category-conditioned model defaults plus an early selection stage that frequently considers competing layouts in prose. The data offer weaker evidence about examples and live reference calibration. Neither the pattern itself nor the face establishes a defect. The [independent copy probe](#functional-regressions-and-independent-checks) quantifies a specific proportion loss while preserving the same Georgia type and cover art.

## First controlled trial

The two briefs cover a [radio rundown](briefs/radio.md) and a [print fair](briefs/print.md). Each has two fresh repetitions of **A** with no UI/design skill, **B** with frozen v3.0.1, and **C** with the isolated change in [selection-experiment.txt](selection-experiment.txt). C changed one file in the local installed payload. Hosted skill discovery and descriptions, shell access and inherited user config were disabled. B and C read their project-scoped `SKILL.md`, manifest and `concept-proof` reference before coding. A had no UI/design skill in its file tree and no skill reads. The baseline manifest digest is `bcf1c8394e8f9dd18b26229c656da77cf9495cd4183359c58c09b936cf7641b5`. C’s is `bafe09fcc9a19378e10136053842f38398beec81fa07d70bf4a600e988715b4a`. Versions and hashes were checked against the exact frozen source. The old user installation was outside the experiment. Each agent had the same 480-second ceiling and scoped browser and file tools. Median elapsed times were 166, 200 and 209 seconds for A, B and C. Model identity, seed and temperature were unavailable in CLI telemetry.

Eight fresh blind reviewers each saw only one shuffled triplet, the brief and the operator tasks. They captured and operated actual apps at desktop and mobile sizes and committed their notes before the assignment key was applied. All twelve concepts and all eight reviews completed. Sixty automatic opening checks covered 1440px, 390px, 320px, reduced motion and doubled root text. There were no page/runtime errors. `27101569e0` showed 1px root overflow at 320px. The text-size probe does not replace browser zoom or AT testing.

| Matched block | Overall preference by reviewer | Visual result | What changed a decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Switchboard 1 | C > A > B and A > B > C | Both ranked C first visually | C’s timeline and inline phone detail connect a slot to the next cue. The revised end stayed in detail, and a repeated activation could collapse the ferry deadline. A preserves the valid Depot selection after the invalid backup. B sometimes made the invalid preview the active choice, requiring another selection. |
| Switchboard 2 | B > A > C twice | Both ranked C first visually | C’s type and ruled rundown read well. Its default mobile detail pushed the fixed boundary lower. Both reviewers independently found that focus moved to Restore after apply and a second Enter reinstated the missing interview. B connected conflict, alternative and recovery while leaving the actual handoff easy to locate. |
| Fold Fair 1 | B > A > C and A > B > C | Both ranked C last visually | C’s poster-like opening had character, but its mobile illustration crowded the title and the mats obscured cover content. B preserved whole covers. A packed more book choices on screen, with a weaker icon-only remove action. |
| Fold Fair 2 | C > B > A twice | One preferred C, one favored A’s tighter print identity | C’s full covers, price and table details, stable focus and a thumbnail shortlist made the browsing sequence work. B’s cobalt banner delayed comparison. A’s textured crop and saved-state heart made identifying and removing an item harder. |

Pairwise across eight reviewer judgments, B led A 5–3 overall. C led B 6–2 visually, 5–3 on interaction and trailed 3–5 overall. C led A 5–3 visually, trailed 3–5 on interaction and 3–5 overall. These are ordinal comparisons with concrete reasons, not scalar aesthetic grades. Two examples show blind weakness detection without a defect-specific hint. Both Fold Fair 1 reviewers called out title and cover cropping in C. Both Switchboard 2 reviewers independently found the repeat-Enter reversal. Reviewers differed over whether detail-only end time was a contract failure or reduced glanceability. The brief requires a visible revised end, which those detail views supply. Missing a duplicate end in the list is a quality issue. An unrelated reversal on repeated Enter is an executed contract failure.

The no-skill radio runs chose dark, sans-led consoles. The two no-skill print runs chose cream, tilted covers and differently styled Georgia and Arial Black headlines. Both print B runs chose Georgia, while radio B used Segoe UI. C’s four outputs used three typographic directions. This sample supports category-conditioned priors and shows that UI Slop does not inevitably produce serif. It does not estimate a model-wide frequency or determine which internal prior caused the choice.

**The manipulation did not take effect.** All four C runs loaded the altered instruction, then recorded that a familiar cue sheet or dialog settled the decision. None preserved an operated counter-direction. The visual preference is a measured association with these four outputs, not evidence that rendered comparisons improved them. Two C agents also saved browser-reviewed or corrected `final/` copies. Those copies and the generators’ rationales remain outside the initial rankings. The measurement is on the unmodified `initial/` entries.

## Follow-up on actual comparison

The [follow-up variant](selection-followup.txt) replaced the same Alternatives section. It explicitly treats conventional interaction mechanics and consequential presentation choices as separate decisions. The brief, runtime, working sizes, budgets, controls and source-selection policy remained the same. All four C runs kept and operated a credible counter-direction at 1440 and 390px. The first drafts were saved before the comparison, so the [first-draft packet](followup-trial/packet/queue.json) measures what each run built first. The [selected-proof packet](selected-proofs/packet/queue.json) measures what each kept after proof. Eight additional fresh reviewers operated the selected packets without access to which app had been revised, the process notes or the first-draft judgments.

| Follow-up C proof | Operated decision and result |
| --- | --- |
| Switchboard 1 · `f2b64dc67c` | Parallel desktop inspector versus inline at both sizes. The inline desktop moved the next cue and evidence below the working viewport. The run retained the original split, and a selected-proof revision brought phone conflict feedback into view after a tap. |
| Switchboard 2 · `538440f88f` | Complete rundown then detail versus inline expansion. In-row detail pushed North Jetty and 19:00 out of view while resolving fit. The selected bytes matched the first draft. The comparison confirmed the initial hypothesis. |
| Fold Fair 1 · `cb0026dd71` | Face-out shelf versus compact catalog. Both used the same art and long Comics title. The shelf was retained. Browser proof led to a title line-break correction, full cover lettering and an initialized Save label. |
| Fold Fair 2 · `a1784a5360` | Cover shelf versus annotated index with synopses. Both paths opened the long Comics record and returned to the same focused title. The shelf was retained. The selected proof trimmed repeated intro copy and contained full cover bounds. |

These runs show that the revised wording can produce the intended exposure. All four retained the first composition pattern, and three changed the selected proof while addressing rendered issues. B and A also corrected some first-draft defects during proof. A better selected proof can reflect the added comparison, ordinary browser inspection or both. The direct evidence for the comparison is strongest when it explicitly verifies the placement or density tradeoff while content and actions stay constant. The experiment did not separately randomize font choice, color, or the amount of browser practice.

C led A 8–0 overall in the selected follow-up proofs, and B led C 5–3 overall, visually, and in interaction. B beat A 6–2 overall although A led visually 6–2. This is evidence that visible identity and operated task quality can favor different options. B’s strengths include direct timing recovery and usefully composed content in several sampled paths. A held its own visually, so the data give no basis for crediting the original skill with a general visual edge. Warm paper, big type and native-sounding role language appear in both. The follow-up no-skill runs used Georgia across both briefs, including the radio runs that used sans in the first trial. The direction was available without the skill and was unstable even within the same category and arm. Available evidence does not identify a baseline instruction whose removal would reliably improve taste.

On the preserved follow-up drafts, B led C 5–3 overall and in interaction, while C led B 6–2 visually. The selected-proof sample reversed the visual preference, while overall preference stayed 5–3. Reviewers marked four of eight C draft judgments Evidence PASS and Quality READY, rising to six with both marks in the selected-proof packet. B also had six selected-proof judgments with both marks. Different reviewers judged those endpoints, which limits direct before-and-after comparisons. Median elapsed generation time was 155 seconds for A, 205 for B and 226 for C within the same 480-second limit. The original follow-up reviewer at slot `1-2` timed out after a capacity error. A fresh blind retry completed the slot with the same app bytes, tasks, and budget. The archive retains both attempts. The follow-up C digest is `a807b859e0ea32ef7d9542046e0937aa36434c9855c52ad3735ee42e86fd429f`.

The selected comparisons give positive evidence for the mechanism’s ability to expose a tradeoff, one strong consumer example and a divided operations example. The effect does not transfer reliably across the two briefs. Two runs per cell limit any inference about long-run preference, and the repeat reviewers on one artifact are independent judgments rather than new generations.

## Functional regressions and independent checks

The [regression runner](regressions.mjs) verifies the archived source hashes and copies the exact Round 2 artifacts to an external evidence directory. It instruments the same Web Lock queue in `86cf04199e` so browser scheduling cannot hide the role race. The lead tab becomes selected while the already-open dialog still shows Withdraw. Closing and reopening restores the lead actions. The fixture-only repair repaints the open dialog when the role commit completes. At 1440, 390 and 320px, the archived variants failed and the repaired variants kept actions, role, shelf area and reloaded state aligned. The trace includes successful recovery and an intentional second action.

In `0b0915fde5`, removing the queued tray moves focus to Mark firing. A second Enter advances the load without a separate decision. The fixture-only repair focuses the load heading and announces the result. A repeat leaves the load Queued. Explicitly focusing and activating Mark firing still advances it. Evidence covers the same three widths, browser Back, restored state on reload and the 1,350 cm² shelf calculation. These are fixture fixes and focused browser regressions. They supply functional evidence independent of visual comparisons.

**Supporting copy.** On the same archived `fc2950d5d4` Initial screen, hiding redundant copy with reserved boxes moved the first item zero pixels. Removing the boxes alone also moved it zero pixels at 1440px because the hero minimum height dominated. Recomposition brought the first item name from about y=865 to y=769. At 390px it moved from y=900 to y=799, putting the name within the 900px test viewport. The art, exact visit facts and controls remained available, and the test opened the first item and returned. This supports testing the reflow after subtraction, as distinct from selecting a new style.

**Changed facts across views.** Both equipment checkout cases confirmed an early 10:20 issue and properly retained a 12:00 reservation. Only the defective variant still showed Available at desk in inventory, including after reload. The second view detects that defect even though confirmation succeeds. Reversal restores availability in both. This validates the probe independently of the composition intervention. The verified repairs and evidence stay in research, with the previously validated skill edits available on their original branches.

## Validation and release decision

The stable branch’s 25 repository tests passed. The packer produced a runtime-only bundle. `skill:doctor` passed for the installed original, including Chromium availability and package integrity. Its installed evidence CLI captured and checksum-validated four normal/reduced smoke states at 1440 and 320px. The 17 browser regression replays passed their expectations, including the expected failures of the original archived apps, successful repaired copies, and the two independent review-process probes. [Reproduction commands](REPRODUCE.md)

| Gate | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence Gate, experiment | **PASS** for local skill verification, snapshot and review receipts, functional regression reproduction and clean installed smoke. The main comparisons remain limited to Chromium and local state. |
| Evidence Gate, product readiness | **FAIL for named cases**, including the repeat-Enter reversal in the first C radio trial and the original archived ceramics faults. Other paths passed checkout, recovery, replacement and browse evidence. Inspect the blind verdicts for each case and the distinction between omitted duplicate data and a false synchronized fact. |
| Quality Gate, adoption | **NOT_READY.** The rendered alternatives changed the quality of the decision record. The selected designs show mixed visual and interaction outcomes and miss the adoption rule in the [frozen protocol](PROTOCOL.md). |

The full-product held-out criterion did not trigger. No experimental guidance was adopted into the runtime and no version was created. Both variants and their exact installs are retained in the sealed research evidence. The prior candidates remain available on their original commits. The demonstrated gains are bounded to the archive repair tests, the explicit same-record check, and reclaimed working space after copy recomposition.
