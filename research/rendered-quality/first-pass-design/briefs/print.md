# Fold Fair

Build an operated concept for browsing independent zines before a small print fair. Readers use it to decide which tables to visit. Original local cover art or cover mockups should help distinguish the publications and describe their content. Visitors need to compare themes, price and pickup context, read about a selected zine, save it, and return to the same browse context to inspect a second one.

| Zine | Topic | Price | Table | Detail |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Night Windows | Photography | $12 | B4 | 24 black-and-white photographs of lit windows on the last tram route. |
| Bus Stop Botany | Field notes | $8 | C7 | Fold-out guide to five plants found at the Crosstown terminus. |
| Three Ways to Set a Table | Comics | $9 | A2 | Three wordless comics about dinner with neighbors. |
| Wet Asphalt | Photography | $16 | B9 | Large-format reflections captured after a summer storm. |
| Free Shelves | Field notes | $6 | C3 | Annotated map of street libraries and a page for new entries. |
| Antenna Club | Comics | $11 | A5 | Short comic about a radio club losing its rooftop access. |

Support discovery, topic filtering, viewing a zine, saving and removing it, and seeing a shortlist. Returning from detail should keep the prior topic and allow continuation to the next item. A reload should retain saves. Cover art may be authored locally using the implementation tools. Purchases, accounts, and actual stock are out of scope. Keep all data and material local.
