# Switchboard

Build an operated concept for the producer taking over a volunteer radio station during its evening changeover. At 18:42, the next fixed on-air boundary is 19:00. The producer needs to reconcile what is ready for air, make one change, and leave a trustworthy schedule for the studio operator. They may review the same rundown on a phone away from the console.

| Time | Segment | Duration | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 18:43 | East Market weather | 0:45 | Ready |
| 18:44 | Harbor ferry suspension notice | 1:20 | Ready, must air before 18:50 |
| 18:46 | Mara Chen on the night bus pilot | 5:30 | Awaiting audio |
| 18:52 | Listener recordings from North Jetty | 3:10 | Ready |
| 19:00 | Station identification and news handoff | 0:30 | Fixed boundary |

Mara's interview can be replaced by the ready 3:45 studio recording “Depot shift before dawn.” Keep the original time and show the revised end time. Offer a reversible change. Include a separate 7:30 backup, “West platforms accessibility call-in,” which cannot fit before the 18:52 segment. Explain the conflict without losing the original selection. Put the current segment identities, readiness, and timing into a runnable sequence with detail, replace, reversal, and continuation to another segment. Restored and changed states must agree wherever the rundown represents them. Auth, actual audio playback, and multi-user storage are out of scope. Keep all data and material local.
