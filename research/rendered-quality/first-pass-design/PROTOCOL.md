# Frozen first-pass design trial

Three arms use the same two briefs, two independent repetitions, local implementation and Playwright Chromium at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844. Assignment is private until review records are sealed. Each agent receives a product brief and a common prototype request. A has no UI/design skill. B receives the frozen original at `3a2e5468a79e5ef5a5bdb3e52445d0fca99cf903`. C gets that same installation with one change to the Alternatives section in `concept-proof.md`. Each session has the same eight-minute ceiling. The stage under study is the first selected, runnable design prior to implementation review. Alternate drafts and rationale are retained separately. The peer reference, prior candidates, archived products, and reviewer criteria remain outside generators' workspaces.

For an initial, blind opening pass, inspect typography and proportion on the screen, status and action hierarchy, redundant copy, and product identity before examining a codebase or rationale. Record the least useful region and why it affects a decision. Operate the shared proof task at desktop and phone. Describe what happens at activation, after completion, on return or reversal, and on the next action. Separate testable contract failures from taste and material design weaknesses. Rank designs within this matched set by observed suitability and use ties when warranted. Include brief front-to-back rationale. Avoid aesthetic scores. Two independent reviewers per triplet use independently shuffled orders.

**Fold Fair path.** Filter to Photography. Open Night Windows, check price and table, save and return by the interface or history. Continue to Wet Asphalt. Open the shortlist, verify Night Windows is present after reload, then remove it. Check the focused action after returning and open a second topic. Try quick repeated save activation and verify the shortlist matches visible state.

**Selection rule.** A candidate warrants a held-out full-product comparison only if it shows a concrete improvement relevant to repeated use, attributable to an operated preselection comparison, in more than one run and across the two briefs, without material correctness losses. The sample cannot establish an overall population preference. Preserve counterexamples, disagreement, timeouts, and exposures.
